PIXN Sync Pro - Chrome Extension (Manifest V3)
=====================================================

⚠️ BEFORE INSTALLING:
Open PIXN Manager in your web browser, open "Server Setup & Configuration",
and click "Save & Apply" at least once!
This writes your verified Web Server Address directly into "server_config.json"
so the extension is pre-configured to your exact server address (NAS, IP, or PC)
before you load it into your browser.

HOW TO INSTALL IN CHROME / EDGE / BRAVE:

1. Click "Save & Apply" in PIXN Setup to generate server_config.json.

2. Open your browser and navigate to:
   chrome://extensions  (or edge://extensions)

3. In the top-right corner, toggle ON:
   [Developer mode]

4. Click the "Load unpacked" button in the top-left corner.

5. Select this "extension" folder.

DONE!
The PIXN Sync Pro extension icon will appear in your browser toolbar with your pre-configured server address.
Whenever you visit https://pixn-retro.dedyn.io, a floating "⚡ Sync to PIXN Pro" button will automatically appear to sync catalogs and auto-import your session cookie with 1 click!
